Bio

“Solve intelligence, then solve everything else.”

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at the National University of Singapore, advised by Prof. Junyuan Hong. Before NUS, I received my M.Eng. from Peking University, advised by Prof. Yasha Wang, and my B.Eng. from Southeast University, after taking the Gaokao two years early at age 16. My previous research focused on multimodality, LLM post-training, agentic systems, and AI safety. My current research interests center on world models and AI interpretability.
